Highland Farms — Ethiopia

Our primary partnerships sit in the Yirgacheffe and Sidamo highlands. Elevation between 2,100 and 2,400 metres produces dense, complex beans with distinctive floral and citrus profiles. We work exclusively with three cooperatives under multi-year direct-trade agreements that guarantee above-fair-trade premiums and community investment in water and schooling.

Selective Harvest

Only fully ripe red cherries are accepted. Pickers return to the same trees multiple times across the harvest window. On arrival at the washing station, cherries undergo three successive density and visual sorts before processing begins.

Washed Process

Depulped cherries ferment in clean mountain spring water for 36–48 hours under controlled temperature. After thorough washing, parchment is dried on raised African beds for 10–14 days until target moisture is reached.

Export & Traceability

Every lot receives a unique identifier that travels from farm plot through export, roasting, and final bag. Guests can scan QR codes on our retail packaging to view harvest date, processing notes, and cupping scores.

Roastery Atelier

Our 15kg Loring S15 Falcon enables precise airflow and temperature control. Each micro-lot receives a custom roast curve developed through iterative cupping. We roast in small batches three times weekly to guarantee peak freshness.

Cup & Serve

Baristas calibrate grind, dose, and yield daily. Water is filtered and mineral-balanced. Every pour-over, espresso, and immersion brew follows documented recipes while leaving room for the barista’s final sensory adjustment.
